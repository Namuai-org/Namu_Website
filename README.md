Namu is an African AI company developing trustworthy voice models for African languages, starting with Hausa. We are building the data, models, and technology needed for AI to understand how people speak, including local accents, cultural context, and low-quality phone audio.

We have developed our first model, Kura, which is designed to understand spoken Hausa, reason about what a person needs, respond naturally through voice, and carry out useful tasks. In our internal evaluations, Kura performs five times better on local context and usability than the other AI models we tested.

Our first use case for Kura is helping organizations make their programs and services more accessible to the communities they serve. Through a simple phone call, someone can speak in Hausa, ask questions, receive trusted information, share feedback, or request support without needing a smartphone or reliable internet.
